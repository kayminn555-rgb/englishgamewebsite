📖 HOW TO PLAY

🖐️
Drag the Hands

Drag the hour hand or minute hand around the clock with your finger or mouse.

🟣
Minute Hand

The long hand tells the minutes. In normal practice, each number represents 5 minutes.

⚫
Hour Hand

The short hand tells the hour and moves smoothly as the minutes change.

🎯
Set the Time

Move both hands until the clock matches the target time, then check your answer.

💡
Use a Hint

Hints help you understand the time without immediately giving away the answer.
