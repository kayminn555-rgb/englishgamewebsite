❓ How to Play

DUAL MATCH

Two teams race to find the one picture that appears in both circles.

1

Look at both circles

Red Team and Blue Team each receive their own group of pictures.

2

Find the match

Exactly one picture is shared by both sides.

3

Tap your side

Tap the matching picture on your own team's side. Mouse and touch both work.

4

Wrong? Keep going!

A wrong picture shakes. If penalty is OFF, no points are lost. If penalty is ON, your team loses 1 point.

5

First correct player wins

The first team to tap the correct picture gets +1. The round then ends immediately.

6

Fastest tap wins

If both players tap at almost the same time, the first valid tap wins the round.

7

Timer

Find the answer before the countdown reaches zero. If time expires, nobody gets a point.

8

Highest score wins

After all rounds, the team with the most points wins the match.
